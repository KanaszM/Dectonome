Encounter: A combat event between one Player party and one Hostile party
Party: A formation of 1 to 6 Units, arranged into a Front Row (up to 3) and a Back Row (up to 3)
	Player: The Party controlled by the user, composed entirely of Allies
	Hostile: The opposing Party, composed entirely of Enemies
Unit: An individual combatant or entity within a Party
	Ally: A Unit belonging to the Player party
	Enemy: A Unit belonging to the Hostile party
Target: Any Unit belonging to the opposing Party relative to the acting Unit
